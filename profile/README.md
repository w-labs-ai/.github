## W Labs
AI for biomedical imaging. Suwon, South Korea · Philippines.

**SkinArch**: AI analysis of line-field confocal OCT (LC-OCT) skin imaging. DEJ segmentation,
3D surface reconstruction and quantified metrics for cosmetic and dermatology research teams.
Research Use Only.

**BrainArch**: AI-assisted brain CT analysis for stroke research. Highlights candidate
lesion regions and separates ischemic from hemorrhagic patterns for researchers
to review and confirm. Research Use Only. Not cleared as a medical device.

Public repositories here are research tools and reference implementations.
Product code and data stay private.
